Calculation Trainer

Every HSC Economics calculation, with new numbers each time and the working shown

The formulas are set out as in the course booklet's “Calculations in Economics” pages.
  • Show all working when a question asks you to calculate: write the formula, substitute, then answer.
  • Do the calculation twice to catch calculator errors.
  • Give units ($, %, index points) and say whether it is an increase or a decrease, a surplus or a deficit.

Every question uses invented figures for a hypothetical economy, as HSC questions do. The “examined” lists are from the HSC papers held in the class Question Finder. For tariff, quota and subsidy diagrams use the Protection Diagram Lab. Your tallies are kept in this browser only.
