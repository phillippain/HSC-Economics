Add the rest of the diagram

    Diagrams are conceptual and use one linear market. The letters follow the protection sheets: A to D are the corners of the tariff revenue rectangle (on a quota diagram they mark the ends of the import gap), and AB is the subsidy per unit at the new quantity. Numbers are in $ or millions of units and are rounded to one decimal place.
