=== er er-1-glob-growth

lv: 3
k: evaluate
Q: Evaluate the impact of globalisation on economic growth and development in emerging and developing economies.
PTS:
- Defines globalisation, growth and development, and treats emerging and developing economies separately
- Trade and foreign investment: export-led growth, technology transfer and jobs, with evidence (e.g. China, India, Vietnam)
- Development: poverty reduction and HDI gains, but uneven distribution and rising inequality within countries
- Costs: volatile capital flows, commodity dependence, environmental damage, exposure to the international business cycle
- A sustained judgement: large benefits for emerging economies; smaller and less certain gains for many developing economies
PLAN:
- Judgement: globalisation has driven growth in emerging economies but its benefits for developing economies are uneven
- Trade and TNCs as engines of growth (evidence and a diagram such as a PPF shift)
- Development outcomes: poverty, HDI, inequality within countries
- Risks: financial flows, commodity prices, environment
- Conclusion weighing growth against development

=== er er-2-cad

Q: Analyse the causes and effects of changes in Australia's current account balance over the last decade.
PTS:
- Describes the trend: deficits, the surpluses of 2019–20 to 2022–23 and the return to deficit from 2023–24
- Cyclical causes: commodity prices and the terms of trade, domestic demand, the exchange rate
- Structural causes: the savings-investment gap and the net primary income deficit from past foreign borrowing and investment
- Effects on foreign liabilities, the exchange rate and external stability
- Uses balance of payments terms correctly and integrates data
PLAN:
- Thesis: recent changes were driven mainly by the terms of trade, against a structural primary income deficit
- The surpluses: commodity prices and the BOGS
- The return to deficit: falling commodity prices and rising imports
- The structural savings-investment gap and net primary income
- Effects on foreign liabilities and external stability; conclusion

Q: Discuss the effects of fluctuations in the value of the Australian dollar on the Australian economy.

- Explains the determination of the exchange rate with a demand and supply diagram for A$

- Diagram and causes of fluctuations
