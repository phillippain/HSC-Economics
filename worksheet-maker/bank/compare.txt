Q: Compare fiscal policy and monetary policy.

Q: Compare the effects of a tariff, a quota and a production subsidy on an import-competing industry.
COLS: Tariff | Quota | Subsidy

- Domestic production => Rises | Rises | Rises

COLS: Fixed (pegged) | Managed (flexible peg) | Floating
- How the rate is set => The government or central bank sets the rate | !The central bank keeps the rate within a band or near a target | Supply and demand in the foreign exchange market
- Role of the central bank => Buys and sells currency to defend the peg | Intervenes when the rate moves outside the band | May intervene rarely to smooth extreme moves
