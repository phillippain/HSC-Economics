Q: A household's weekly income rises from $1,000 to $1,200 and its weekly consumption rises from $900 to $1,050. Its marginal propensity to consume (MPC) is
A: 0.75
B: 0.875
C: 0.25
D: 1.33
ANS: A
WHY: MPC = change in consumption ÷ change in income = 150 ÷ 200 = 0.75. B is consumption ÷ income at the new level, the average, not the marginal, propensity.

A: 0.8
B: 1.8
C: 0.2

ANS: C
WHY: Every extra dollar of income is either spent or saved, so MPC + MPS = 1 and MPS = 1 − 0.8 = 0.2.
