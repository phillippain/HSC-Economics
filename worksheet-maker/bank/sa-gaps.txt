=== sa sa-1A2-01
c: 1A.2
m: 3
lv: 2
k: explain
Q: Explain why Gross World Product is usually compared using purchasing power parity rather than market exchange rates.
PTS:
- GWP adds up the output of all economies, which must be converted to a common currency (1)
- Market exchange rates do not reflect differences in what money buys in each country (1)
- PPP adjusts for price differences, so it gives a better comparison of real output, raising the measured share of emerging and developing economies (1)

Q: Explain why global financial flows have grown faster than world trade.
PTS:
- Deregulation of financial markets and removal of capital controls since the 1980s (1)
- Technology lets funds move instantly and cheaply around the world (1)
- Investors seek the highest returns and spread risk, and floating exchange rates create speculation and hedging (1)

Q: Describe the phases of the business cycle.
PTS:
- Upswing (recovery and boom): rising growth, falling unemployment, rising inflation (1)

- Trough: lowest point; a recession is two consecutive quarters of negative growth (1)
