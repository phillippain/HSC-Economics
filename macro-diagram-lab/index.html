Shift the curves, predict the result, and write the sentence that explains the diagram

Curve labels

These diagrams are conceptual and not to scale: the numbers behind them only keep the curves consistent. In an exam, draw large, give the diagram a title, label both axes and every curve, mark the before and after points, and refer to the diagram in your writing before you draw it.
