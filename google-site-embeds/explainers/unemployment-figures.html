Figure 5. Cyclical unemployment: aggregate demand falls

AD1 to AD2 lowers output from Y1 to Y2. Firms need fewer workers, so unemployment rises. Not to scale.

Figure 6. Unemployment and inflation: the Phillips curve

The Phillips curve (PC) slopes down: lower unemployment is associated with higher inflation, and the shaded band marks the 2–3% target. Not to scale.
