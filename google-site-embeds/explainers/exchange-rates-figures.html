Figure 6. How a depreciation works through the economy

The Australian dollar depreciates
↓
Exports are cheaper overseas and imports are dearer here
↓
Export volumes rise and import volumes fall
↓
Net exports and aggregate demand rise
↓
Growth and employment rise
↓
Import prices add to inflation
