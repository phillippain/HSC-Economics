Figure 4. A supply shock: policy trade-off

A rise in costs shifts SRAS to the left: the price level rises (P1 to P2) and real GDP falls (Y1 to Y2). Tightening policy to cut prices deepens the fall in output; stimulating demand to restore output raises prices further. Not to scale.
