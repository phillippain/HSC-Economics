Figure 3. Contractionary monetary policy

A higher cash rate shifts AD to the left: real GDP falls (Y1 to Y2) and the price level is lower (P1 to P2) than it would have been. Not to scale.

Figure 4. Chain: RBA raises the cash rate

RBA raises the cash rate target
↓

↓
Spending and investment fall; AUD may rise
↓
Aggregate demand slows
↓
Inflation falls, but unemployment may rise
