Figure 2. The effect of a tariff

A tariff lifts the price from Pw to Pw+t. Local output rises from Q1 to Q2, demand falls from Q4 to Q3 and imports shrink. Removing the tariff reverses this. Not to scale.

Figure 3. Tariff on imported passenger cars (%)

Rates fell in yearly steps between the dates shown. The rate has been 5% since 2010.
