Figure 5. How a slowdown in China reaches Australia

China's growth slows and it builds less
↓
China makes less steel and buys less iron ore
↓
The iron ore price falls
↓
Australia's export income and terms of trade fall
↓
Mining profits and company tax receipts fall
↓
National income grows more slowly and the dollar tends to depreciate
