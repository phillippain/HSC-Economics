Figure 6. How the multiplier works

Firms invest an extra $10 billion
↓
Workers and suppliers earn $10 billion more
↓
With an MPC of 0.8 they spend $8 billion of it
↓
That spending is income for others, who spend $6.4 billion
↓
The rounds continue, each smaller than the last
↓
National income rises by $50 billion in total
