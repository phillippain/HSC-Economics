The economics paragraph

The paragraph structure from the course booklet. Use it for every body paragraph in Sections III and IV.

Repeat E → D → E. Two or three cycles a paragraph; each must add a new mechanism or new data.

Use the stimulus. In Section III, cite the stimulus by its source in at least one cycle of every paragraph.

A diagram earns marks only when it is accurate, labelled and explained in the paragraph around it. Refer to it in the text before you draw it.

What the directive asks for

The key words in a question tell you what kind of answer earns the marks. Many questions have more than one.

Short answers: Section II

Marks and lines show how much depth is needed. Use the stimulus when there is one, and show all working in a calculation.

Outline

General, big-picture information. An opening statement, then one clear point for each mark.

Explain

Show how or why, linking cause and effect. An opening statement or definition, then for each point: a description, the effect (why or how) and an example. Finish with one or two sentences linking back to the question.

Words that show cause and effect

  • because
  • since
  • therefore
  • consequently
  • as a result
  • for this reason
  • subsequently
  • this means
  • leads to
  • results in

The whole response: Sections III and IV

Plan first. About 35 minutes for each response, including the plan. Aim for about four pages.

Introduction. Define the key terms from the question and state your point of view. Do not just restate the question.

Body. One major idea in each paragraph, fully explained, developed and supported. Go back to the wording of the question regularly.

Conclusion. Draw the strands of your argument together. Nothing at odds with the body, and no new evidence.

Data. A top-band response relates the theory to what is happening now in the Australian and global economy, with accurate, dated statistics.

Diagrams you can use

The builder suggests diagrams that fit the question. Draw large, title it, label both axes and every curve, and mark the before and after points.
